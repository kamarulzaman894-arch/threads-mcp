KZKZ Threads MCP

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026  ·  Last updated: 8 October 2026

KZ Threads MCP is an independently operated integration for connecting an authorized Threads account to content research, reporting and owner-controlled content workflows. This policy explains what information the integration may access and how it is handled. The operator is based in Malaysia. For privacy inquiries, email kamarulzaman894@gmail.com.

1. Information we process

Depending on which Threads API permissions are granted and which functions are used, the service may process:

  • Threads account identifiers and public-facing profile details, such as username, name, biography and profile picture.
  • Threads posts, text, media metadata, links, timestamps, replies, conversations and mentions available through permitted API endpoints.
  • Insights and engagement information available for the connected account, such as views, likes, replies, reposts and related performance metrics.
  • Keyword searches, public profile lookup results and location-related information, only where Meta grants the necessary access.
  • OAuth authorizations, access credentials, authorization/session metadata and technical information needed to keep the connection working.
  • Where the separate staging portal is used, the login email, post drafts, revision history, approval decisions and requested posting schedules.
  • Basic operational and security information that may be generated by infrastructure providers, such as request timestamps, error information and IP addresses.

2. How we use information

We use this information to authenticate the account owner; retrieve authorized Threads data; analyze posts, content and engagement; support keyword research within permitted access; prepare reports and drafts; diagnose errors; protect the service; and support owner-controlled scheduling or publishing workflows if those capabilities are separately enabled and authorized.

Current production behavior: The remote ChatGPT MCP connection is operated in read-only mode. Scheduling and draft work in a separate staging environment do not mean autonomous production publishing is enabled. Public keyword discovery is subject to Meta's access approval and actual API availability.

3. Authorization and permissions

Threads access is provided using Meta's official authorization process. The service requests permissions relevant to its configured features, which may include basic profile access, insights, replies, mentions, keyword search, profile discovery, location features and publishing or management permissions for separately approved workflows. A requested permission is not a claim that Meta has approved public or advanced access, or that a related feature is active.

Only information made available through the authorized API and the owner's permitted activities is used. We do not ask users to disclose their Threads password to this service.

4. Storage and retention

Authentication tokens and certain OAuth connection records may be kept in application-managed storage while needed to maintain the connection. OAuth session records can have defined expiration times. Long-lived Threads credentials are checked for expiration when loaded but may remain in backing storage until replaced or deleted. Retrieved Threads content is primarily used to answer requests; service memory, logs and optional staging records may persist for different periods.

Staging draft and scheduling records may remain until they are deleted or the staging environment is retired. We do not promise immediate automatic deletion of every record when access expires. Requests for deletion can be sent using the contact information below. Some minimal records may need to be retained temporarily for security, troubleshooting or legal obligations.

5. Providers and disclosures

Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the integration, including Meta/Threads (the source API), Render (application hosting), an application data store used for OAuth persistence, Supabase (optional staging authentication and draft data), and ChatGPT/OpenAI when the owner invokes connected Threads tools through ChatGPT. Their handling of information is also governed by their own applicable privacy terms.

We do not sell Threads data or use it to build advertising audiences or target unrelated advertising. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect the security and integrity of the service.

6. Security

We use account authorization, authenticated service endpoints, access checks and transport encryption where supported by the service infrastructure. However, no online system or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Access tokens and sensitive connection information should never be pasted into public posts or support requests.

7. Choices, access and deletion requests

The connected account owner can revoke the integration's Threads permissions through the appropriate Meta or Threads connected-app settings. To ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of data held by KZ Threads MCP, email kamarulzaman894@gmail.com with the subject KZ Threads MCP - Privacy Request and a brief description of the request. Do not include passwords or access tokens. We may ask for reasonable verification before acting and will respond in accordance with applicable law.

Revoking access may prevent future API requests but may not itself erase copies of records already stored by the integration. Please request deletion directly if this is needed. Deletion requests do not alter data retained independently by Meta or other third parties.

8. Children

The integration is intended for business and professional use and is not designed to collect personal information directly from children. If you believe that such information has been provided to us, please contact us to request review and deletion.

9. International processing

The operator is based in Malaysia. Hosting and integration providers may process data in other countries according to their own infrastructure and terms. Applicable data protection rights may vary by location.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the service, permissions or data practices change. Material changes will be reflected by an updated date on this public page. Continued access to a feature is subject to the applicable authorization and privacy requirements.
